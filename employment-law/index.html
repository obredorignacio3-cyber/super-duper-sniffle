You Have Rights at Work

✓
Right to fair pay: Minimum wage, overtime, and timely payment are legally required.
✓
Right to a safe workplace: Employers must provide a workplace free from recognized hazards.
✓
Right to be free from discrimination: Hiring, promotion, and termination decisions cannot be based on protected characteristics.
✓
Right to report violations: You cannot be retaliated against for reporting illegal activity or safety concerns.
✓
Right to organize: You can discuss wages, working conditions, and join or support a union.
